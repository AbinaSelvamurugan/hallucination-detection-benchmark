# Generated results

Full research runs and downloaded datasets are gitignored by default so restricted content is not published accidentally. Only `fixture-smoke/` and `fixture_threshold.json` are committed, as deterministic offline examples.

The committed examples come from the manually authored unit-test fixtures, so they are not public-dataset measurements or results on HaluEval and RAGTruth. To evaluate a real benchmark, follow the workflow in `README.md`, check the labels and dataset licensing, and generate fresh experiment output in your local `results/` directory.
